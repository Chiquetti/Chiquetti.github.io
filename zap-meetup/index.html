(~70 sec) Here's how Zap prospects — watch him roll through four steps.

One: he searches the web and finds businesses to prospect. Two: he runs a technical audit on each one — their website, their Google reviews, and their social channels: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube.

Three — and this is the clever part — he keeps only the under-performers. If a business is already crushing it online, they probably already have an agency. So Zap focuses on the ones with real room to grow.

Four: he writes a personalized, positive message. No "your website is bad." Instead, we lead with value — a free sample, what we call an AI Visibility Check.

(~80 sec) Now the real numbers — one single night of work. Everything starts at zero. Let me hit "Run Zap." [click ▶ Run Zap — the numbers count up in funnel order and the belt starts]

Zap and his bot crew scraped more than 1,300 business records, from 24 sources: Brazilian business directories, business expos, and 192 separate Google Maps searches. After removing duplicates, that's 739 unique businesses.

Then the filtering. 425 were technically audited. 130 were thrown out — duplicates, closed, not a fit, or already strong online. About 60 were hand-checked in a real browser. And at the end: 23 verified, high-fit leads in Zoho CRM — each with an audit report, a personalized message, and two follow-up tasks. That's 46 tasks.

The point isn't the 23. The point is we scanned everything, and kept only the best — so we never waste time on the wrong lead. All overnight.

(~55 sec) Speed means nothing if your CRM turns into a mess. So Zap has an auditor bot — watch him check every item, one by one.

Duplicates are checked before anything is created. Every lead gets tags and UTM tracking, so I always know where it came from. Contacts are verified in a real browser — not guessed.

And the most important rule of all: nothing invented. If the bots can't verify a piece of information, it simply doesn't go in. With AI, that rule is everything.

Down here is the weekly rhythm: every morning at 8 AM I get a briefing. Three times every weekday the bots check for replies. Follow-ups are prepared the day before they're due. Mondays bring a fresh batch of new leads, and Fridays a weekly report. A sales routine that never takes a day off.
